# Day 12 – Linear Algebra Fundamentals: Vectors and Matrices

## Topics Covered
- Introduction to Linear Algebra.
- Understanding Scalars and Vectors.
- Introduction to Matrices.
- Types and dimensions of matrices.
- Basic matrix operations.
- Applications of vectors and matrices in Machine Learning.

## Work Performed

Today's session focused on the fundamentals of Linear Algebra and its importance in Machine Learning. We started with an introduction to scalars, vectors and matrices and discussed how they are used to represent numerical data.

We learned how vectors store values in an ordered form and how matrices organise data into rows and columns. Different types of matrices and their dimensions were also discussed.

The session covered basic matrix operations such as addition, subtraction and multiplication. We also discussed how vectors and matrices are used in Machine Learning algorithms for representing datasets and performing mathematical computations.

## Learning Outcomes

- Understood the basic concepts of Linear Algebra.
- Learned the difference between scalars, vectors and matrices.
- Became familiar with matrix dimensions and types.
- Understood basic matrix operations.
- Learned the importance of Linear Algebra in Machine Learning.

## Conclusion

The session provided a basic understanding of vectors and matrices and their role in mathematical computations. These concepts form an important foundation for understanding Machine Learning algorithms and Deep Learning.
