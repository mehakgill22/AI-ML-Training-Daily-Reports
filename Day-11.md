# Day 11 – Probability Concepts for Machine Learning

## Topics Covered
- Introduction to Probability.
- Basic probability rules.
- Understanding Events and Outcomes.
- Independent and Dependent Events.
- Conditional Probability.
- Importance of Probability in Machine Learning.

## Work Performed

Today's session focused on understanding the basic concepts of Probability and their importance in Machine Learning. We discussed how probability is used to measure the likelihood of an event occurring and how it helps in dealing with uncertainty.

We learned about events, outcomes and basic probability rules with simple examples. The session also covered independent and dependent events and how the occurrence of one event can affect another.

We were also introduced to Conditional Probability, which explains the probability of an event occurring when another event has already happened. Different examples were discussed to understand how probability concepts are useful in prediction and decision-making.

## Learning Outcomes

- Understood the basic concepts of Probability.
- Learned about events, outcomes and probability rules.
- Understood independent and dependent events.
- Became familiar with Conditional Probability.
- Learned how probability helps in handling uncertainty in Machine Learning.

## Conclusion

The session provided a basic understanding of probability and its applications in Machine Learning. It helped explain how probability concepts are useful for making predictions and working with uncertain data.
