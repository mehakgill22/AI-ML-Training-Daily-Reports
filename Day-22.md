# Day 22 – Neural Networks and Deep Learning

## Topics Covered
Today’s session focused on Neural Networks, an important part of Deep Learning. We learned how neural networks are inspired by the working of the human brain and how they process information through interconnected layers. We also studied activation functions, backpropagation and popular deep learning frameworks such as TensorFlow and PyTorch.

## Work Performed
- Studied the basic structure of an Artificial Neural Network.
- Learned about the input layer, hidden layers and output layer.
- Understood how neurons are connected through weights and biases.
- Explored activation functions such as ReLU, Sigmoid and Tanh and their role in neural networks.
- Studied the concept of backpropagation and how it helps update weights to reduce prediction errors.
- Learned about the importance of training a neural network using data.
- Got introduced to TensorFlow and PyTorch, which are commonly used frameworks for building and training neural networks.

## Learning Outcomes
- Understood the basic architecture of neural networks.
- Learned the purpose of different layers and neurons.
- Understood how activation functions help neural networks learn complex patterns.
- Learned the basic concept of backpropagation.
- Became familiar with TensorFlow and PyTorch and their use in deep learning.

## Conclusion
Today’s session helped me understand the fundamentals of neural networks and deep learning. I learned how neural networks process information, improve predictions through backpropagation and use frameworks like TensorFlow and PyTorch for model development.
