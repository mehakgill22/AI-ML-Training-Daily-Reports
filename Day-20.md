# Day 20 – Clustering and K-Means Algorithm

## Topics Covered
Today’s session focused on clustering, an unsupervised machine learning technique used to group similar data points. We also studied the K-Means Clustering Algorithm and how it divides a dataset into different groups based on similarities.

## Work Performed
- Understood the concept of clustering and its role in unsupervised learning.
- Learned how clustering groups similar data points without predefined labels.
- Studied different applications of clustering, such as customer segmentation, image grouping and market analysis.
- Explored the working of the K-Means Clustering Algorithm.
- Learned about the role of centroids in forming clusters.
- Understood the basic steps of K-Means, including selecting the number of clusters, assigning data points and updating centroids.
- Discussed how the value of K affects the final clustering results.

## Learning Outcomes
- Understood the basic concept of unsupervised learning and clustering.
- Learned how K-Means divides data into groups.
- Understood the importance of centroids in clustering.
- Identified some real-world applications of clustering techniques.

## Conclusion
Today’s session helped me understand how clustering is used to find patterns and group similar data. I also learned the basic working of K-Means and how it helps organize large datasets into meaningful clusters.
