## Day 1 – Introduction to Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Deep Learning

## Topics Covered
- Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- Basics of Machine Learning (ML)
- Introduction to Deep Learning
- Difference between AI, ML and DL
- Real-world applications of AI and ML

## Work Performed

The first day of training started with an introduction to Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. We discussed what AI is and how it helps computers perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

We also learned about Machine Learning and how computers use data to identify patterns and make predictions. After that, Deep Learning was introduced, along with its connection to Machine Learning.

The session also covered the basic differences between AI, ML and Deep Learning. Some real-life examples, such as voice assistants, recommendation systems, face recognition and chatbots, were discussed to understand their practical uses.

## Learning Outcomes

- Understood the basic concepts of AI, ML and Deep Learning.
- Learned how Machine Learning uses data for making predictions.
- Understood the relationship between the three technologies.
- Became familiar with some common applications of AI in daily life.

## Conclusion

The first session provided a good introduction to the field of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning and helped build a foundation for the upcoming topics.
