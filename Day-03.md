# Day 3 – Real-World Applications and Use Cases of AI/ML

## Topics Covered
- Applications of Artificial Intelligence in daily life.
- Use of Machine Learning in different industries.
- AI-based recommendation systems.
- Chatbots and virtual assistants.
- Applications of AI in healthcare, education and transportation.

## Work Performed

Today’s session focused on understanding how Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning are used in real-world situations. We discussed different applications of these technologies and how they make various tasks easier and more efficient.

We explored recommendation systems used by platforms like YouTube and Netflix, which suggest content based on users' interests. We also discussed chatbots and virtual assistants that help users by answering questions and providing information.

Some applications of AI in healthcare, education, banking and transportation were also discussed. The session helped in understanding how Machine Learning can analyse data, identify patterns and support decision-making in different fields.

## Learning Outcomes

- Learned about different real-world applications of AI and ML.
- Understood how recommendation systems work at a basic level.
- Became familiar with the use of chatbots and virtual assistants.
- Explored the role of AI in different industries.
- Understood the importance of AI in solving practical problems.

## Conclusion

The session provided an overview of how AI and Machine Learning are being used in everyday life and different industries. It helped connect the theoretical concepts of AI and ML with their practical applications.
